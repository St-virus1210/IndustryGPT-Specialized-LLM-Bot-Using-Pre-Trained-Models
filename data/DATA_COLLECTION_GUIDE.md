# Data Collection Guide

Use this as the collection checklist before final submission.

## Preferred sources
- Official university/institution FAQs
- Student handbooks
- Academic regulations
- Examination regulations
- Library rules
- Student-services pages
- Government/open education resources
- Public research datasets

## For each source record
- source name
- URL
- access date
- license/usage terms
- extracted topic/category
- original text or question
- cleaned answer
- citation/reference

## Ethics and legality
The supplied brief requires ethical and legal extraction and adherence to privacy and usage policies. Do not include personal student information or private chat logs without appropriate authorization.
